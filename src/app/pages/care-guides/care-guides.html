Betta Care

Betta Fish Care Guide

Learn how to properly care for your betta fish — from tank setup and feeding to maintenance and health monitoring.

Tank Setup

Use at least a 5-gallon tank with a heater and gentle filter. Add plants and hiding spots to keep your betta comfortable.

Water Conditions

Maintain clean, dechlorinated water at 24–28°C. Regularly check ammonia, nitrites, and nitrate levels.

Feeding

Feed high-quality pellets once or twice daily. Avoid overfeeding and occasionally give treats like bloodworms.

Maintenance

Perform weekly water changes and clean the tank regularly to prevent harmful buildup.

Health

Watch for signs of illness such as torn fins, inactivity, or color loss. Early detection is key.

Behavior

Bettas are territorial and prefer living alone. Provide a calm and enriched environment.

Daily Care Checklist

Check water temperature
Feed proper amount
Observe behavior
Remove leftover food
