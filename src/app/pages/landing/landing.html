Introduction

Why Betta Fish Need Proper Care

Betta fish may be small, but they still need clean water, proper feeding, enough swimming space, and a calm environment. Without the right care, they can become stressed and develop health problems.

Key Facts

  • Known for bright colors and elegant fins
  • Territorial and often kept alone
  • Need clean, warm, stable water
  • Require a proper feeding routine
