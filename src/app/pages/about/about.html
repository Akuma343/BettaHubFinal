BettaHub: A better way to learn betta care

BettaHub is an educational website designed to make betta fish care easier to understand for beginners, hobbyists, and pet enthusiasts.

Project Background

Many betta fish care resources are scattered across forums, social posts, and short-form videos. BettaHub organizes that information into simple, structured sections that are easier to follow.

  • Clear learning structure
  • Simple and responsive layout
  • Front-end only educational website

Purpose of BettaHub

BettaHub aims to provide reliable and beginner-friendly guidance on habitat setup, feeding, water care, health, and behavior.

The goal is to help users care for betta fish responsibly by making important information easy to read, practical, and accessible.
